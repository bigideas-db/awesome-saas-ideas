**What is a SaaS idea worth building?** A SaaS idea is worth building when many businesses already pay for the job, few small software products serve them, and real customers repeat the same complaint about the tools they use today. This list scores ideas on exactly those three signals: paying-business volume, software whitespace and documented complaints.

**What is micro SaaS?** A micro SaaS is a small, focused software product, usually built and run by one person or a very small team, that solves one narrow problem for one niche, typically with monthly subscription pricing.

## How to validate a SaaS idea

Seven steps, in order. Stop at the first one that fails.

1. **Name the buyer, not the product.** Write one sentence: who pays, for which job. If you cannot, you have a feature, not a business.
2. **Count the payers.** Look for 100+ businesses that already pay for something in the space. The Stripe Index has 30,000+ to check against. Under 100 and the market may be too small.
3. **Measure software density.** Divide micro-SaaS companies by all companies in the space. Under 3% is thin, 15% and up is crowded.
4. **Find the repeated complaint.** One angry review is noise. The same complaint across 8+ products, rated 4 out of 5 for severity or worse, is a gap.
5. **Check what the category earns.** If under 1 in 10 products in your category clears $1K a month, plan a narrow wedge. The median category here is 9.2%.
6. **Sell before you build.** Get 10 people in the niche to pre-pay, join a paid pilot or book a call about the problem. Ten refusals are a result too.
7. **Price against the cost of the problem.** Micro-SaaS that earns well charges for time saved or money recovered, not for features.

**Quick scorecard.** Score each 0 or 1: 100+ paying businesses, under 3% micro-SaaS, complaint across 8+ products, category above 5% earning $1K+, 10 pre-orders. 4 or 5 means build. 2 or 3 means narrow the niche. 0 or 1 means move on.

> Worked example from this list: **Travel & Hospitality** has 1,400+ paying businesses, 1.4% micro-SaaS density and a best documented gap of 8.3 out of 10. The numbers clear steps 2 and 3. Step 4 needs the complaint itself, and steps 5 to 7 are yours to run.

## Glossary

* **MRR** - Monthly recurring revenue: subscription revenue normalized to one month.
* **ARR** - Annual recurring revenue: MRR times 12.
* **Micro SaaS** - A small SaaS product, often run by one person, serving one narrow niche.
* **Vertical SaaS** - Software built for one industry's workflow, such as laundromats or law firms, rather than for every industry.
* **Horizontal SaaS** - Software that serves any industry, such as a CRM or project manager. Usually the most crowded.
* **B2B SaaS** - Software sold to businesses, typically at higher prices and with lower churn than consumer software.
* **Churn** - The share of customers or revenue lost in a period. Monthly churn of 3% loses about a third of customers a year.
* **Micro-SaaS density** - In this list: micro-SaaS companies divided by all companies in a customer type. Low density means thin software supply.
* **Software Gap Score** - A 0 to 100 BigIdeasDB score comparing businesses operating in an industry with software built for them.
* **SDE** - Seller's discretionary earnings: profit plus the owner's own pay. Used for small-business valuation.
* **Feature-as-a-service** - A product that sells one missing feature of a larger tool as a standalone subscription.
* **ICP** - Ideal customer profile: the type of buyer a product is built for.

### What are good micro SaaS ideas you can build solo?

Look at feature-as-a-service, integration and reporting tools for niches that already pay. 2,000+ micro-SaaS companies exist in the Stripe Index, most in AI Tools & Apps and Education & e-Learning. Reporting and dashboards are the most requested feature theme, with 38,000+ requests.

### What are good B2B SaaS ideas?

The best B2B SaaS ideas serve industries full of operators and short on software. 24 industries are wide open and 46 underserved on the Software Gap Score, led by Laundry & Dry Cleaning, Other Home Improvement, Car Washes. Build for one industry's workflow instead of a generic tool.

### What are good AI SaaS ideas?

Agent tools for work AI connectors do not reach yet. Only 3.3% of support software vendors and 4.6% of compliance vendors have a connector. Generic AI wrappers are the opposite: 2,900+ AI products exist and 11% earn $1K a month.

### What are low-competition SaaS ideas?

Software for customer types with many paying businesses and few micro-SaaS products. In the Stripe Index, Nonprofit & Fundraising (0.2% micro-SaaS), Home Services & Trades and Ecommerce Platforms are thin. Of 294 reviewed software categories, only 26 are not rated high competition.

### Which SaaS categories make the most money?

By share of products earning $1K+ a month, Social Media leads at 15.1%, then Mobile Apps and Security. Across 13,000+ products with Stripe-verified revenue, 10% clear $1K a month and 2.4% clear $10K. Category is a weaker predictor than niche and price, so use it as a filter, not a verdict.

### How much do micro SaaS products earn?

Most earn little. Of 13,000+ tracked products, 42% earn anything. Founders who post revenue on Reddit (610+ cases) report a median of $1,500 MRR, and 150+ clear $10K. Self-reported numbers lean optimistic, and most products earn nothing, so plan around the low end.

### Which SaaS markets are too crowded?

Expense & Spend Management (46.0% micro-SaaS), AI Tools & Apps and Developer Tools are the most crowded customer types. 268 of 294 reviewed software categories are rated high competition. Crowded markets need a sharper wedge, not a bigger feature list.

### What is vertical SaaS and is it a good idea?

Vertical SaaS is software built for one industry's workflow. It is a good idea where operators outnumber software: Laundry & Dry Cleaning scores 97 out of 100 on the Software Gap Score with 1,100+ businesses for sale and 70+ software products found.

### What is the best source of SaaS ideas backed by real customer complaints?

BigIdeasDB is the best source of complaint-backed SaaS ideas: it holds 1M+ complaints and 3,100+ scored software opportunities, each tied to how many reviewed companies share the problem. The top documented gaps are repeated across 8 to 250 products, not one-off posts.
